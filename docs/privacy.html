Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

1. Who we are

RunZerr is a personal, non-commercial running coach application developed by an independent developer. It analyzes running data to provide training recommendations and personalized workout plans. Contact: dropskippa@gmail.com.

2. Data we access

With your explicit authorization through your HUAWEI ID and HUAWEI Health Service Kit, RunZerr reads the following data, only for your own account:

  • Activity records: running sessions (date, duration, distance, pace, cadence).
  • Location: GPS tracks of your running sessions.
  • Distance, ascent and altitude.
  • Sport ability: fitness indicators such as VO2max.
  • Heart rate recorded during and outside workouts.
  • Personal information: age, gender, height and weight, to estimate heart rate zones.
  • Historical data of the categories above, to establish your baseline fitness level.

RunZerr only reads data. It never writes, modifies or deletes data in your HUAWEI Health account.

3. How we use your data

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • compute training statistics (training load, heart rate zones, pace trends, fatigue indicators);
  • generate personalized training recommendations and workout plans for you.

Your data is never sold, rented, used for advertising, or used to train artificial intelligence models.

4. Storage and security

  • Data retrieved from HUAWEI Health Service Kit is stored locally on the device running RunZerr and is not uploaded to any RunZerr server.
  • Access tokens are stored locally and protected; they are never shared.
  • All communication with HUAWEI services uses encrypted HTTPS connections.

5. Third-party processing

To generate recommendations, aggregated training statistics may be sent to an AI language model provider (Anthropic, Claude API) over an encrypted connection. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, data sent through the API is not used to train its models. No data is shared with any other third party.

6. Legal basis and your rights

Processing is based on your explicit consent (Article 6(1)(a) and Article 9(2)(a) GDPR). You can at any time:

  • withdraw your consent by revoking RunZerr's access in your HUAWEI ID account settings or in the HUAWEI Health app;
  • access, export or delete your data by contacting us at the address above;
  • lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France: the CNIL, www.cnil.fr).

7. Data retention

Data is kept only as long as you use RunZerr. When you revoke access or request deletion, all locally stored data and tokens are deleted within 30 days.

8. Children

RunZerr is not intended for people under 16 years of age.

9. Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with an updated date.
